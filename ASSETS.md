# Asset sources and licenses

## 3D models (`src/assets/models/`)

All current models are original to this project. They are generated by the Blender scripts in `assets-src/blender/`. Rebuild them with `npm run assets` (see the script header for the Blender-as-Python setup). No third-party model files are included.

| File | Contents | Source | License |
|---|---|---|---|
| `windmill.glb` | Stone tower, island, door, windows, shingled cap, lattice sails (`Sails`), oak sweepers matching the spinner collider (`Sweepers`), cobbled pond edge | `assets-src/blender/windmill.py` | Project-owned |
| `walls.glb` | Modular wall kit: stone and brick sections and rounded ends, oak caps with brass bolts | `assets-src/blender/props.py` | Project-owned |
| `cup.glb` | Brass-rimmed cup with a deep liner | `assets-src/blender/props.py` | Project-owned |
| `lantern.glb` | Mahogany corner post with a brass carriage lantern | `assets-src/blender/props.py` | Project-owned |
| `decor.glb` | Oak barrel with iron hoops; oak planter with brass caps | `assets-src/blender/props.py` | Project-owned |
| `rocks.glb` | Three small rock clusters | `assets-src/blender/props.py` | Project-owned |
| `boulders.glb` | Three boulders used for rock obstacles | `assets-src/blender/props.py` | Project-owned |
| `garden.glb` | Timber edging and corner posts, clipped hedge, tree, flower, stepping stones, lamp post | `assets-src/blender/garden.py` | Project-owned |

Textures (turf, lawn, leaves, woods, rock, clay, dirt, ash, lava, dinosaur skin) and the skies are generated procedurally at runtime (`src/art.ts`, `src/garden.ts`, `src/jurassic.ts`). The cartoon dinosaurs, volcanoes, tumbleweeds, palms, bushes, bones and nests are built in code in `src/jurassic.ts`, and the aliens, astronauts, asteroids, planets, rocket, UFO and crystals in `src/space.ts`. Ball and cue designs are painted in code in `src/gear.ts` (all project-owned).
