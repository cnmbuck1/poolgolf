Support

Rack & Roll, by Cnmbuck Studios

Contact

The quickest way to reach us is the speech-bubble button on the game's main menu (or Pause → Report a bug / feedback during a hole). It tells us which hole you were on.

Or email us at cnmbuckstudios@gmail.com. We usually reply within a few days.

How to play

  • Pull back anywhere on the screen to draw the cue (further for more power), drag sideways to aim, and let go to shoot.
  • Use the spin pad (bottom right) to curve the ball around obstacles, stop it sooner, or roll it further.
  • Tap the light bulb during a hole for tips on that hole, or Watch how to play on the main menu.

Common questions

Where did my progress go? Progress is saved on your device. It isn't shared between devices, and deleting the app deletes it.

How do I stop sending play statistics? Settings → Share anonymous play stats.
