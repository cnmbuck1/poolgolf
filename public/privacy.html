Privacy policy

Rack & Roll, by Cnmbuck Studios · Last updated 26 October 2026

Rack & Roll is a mini-golf game played with a pool cue. The app is free and shows ads, which you can turn off with a one-time purchase. We collect as little as possible ourselves and never sell anything.

What stays on your device

Your coins, unlocked balls and cues, scores, stars, trophies and settings are saved on your device only. Deleting the app deletes them.

Anonymous play statistics

To find holes that are too hard or too easy, the game sends a short record when you finish a hole: which course and hole, par, your strokes, how long it took, the game mode, the game's version, and the kind of device (for example "iPhone 844x390"). Each device gets a random ID so one player's holes can be told apart. If the game hits an unexpected error, it also sends the error message and where in the game it happened, so we can fix it. These records contain no name, email, location, contacts or advertising identifiers, and are not linked to you.

You can turn this off at any time: Settings → Share anonymous play stats.

Ads

The app shows ads from Google AdMob: an optional video you can choose to watch for coins, and, unless you buy Remove ads, an occasional full-screen ad between rounds. To show and measure ads, Google collects information such as your device's advertising identifier (only if you allow it when the app asks to track), IP address, rough location derived from it, device type and ad interactions. If you don't allow tracking, you still see ads, just less personalised ones. Players in the EU, UK and similar regions are asked for consent first and can change their choice at any time in Settings → Ad privacy choices. Google's use of this data is described at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Purchases

Remove ads is bought through Apple. We don't see your payment details; Apple tells the app only that the purchase was made, so it can be restored on a new device with Settings → Restore purchases.

Reminders

If you turn on the daily challenge reminder, the reminder is scheduled on your device. Nothing is sent to us.

Feedback you send

If you use the feedback button, we receive what you type, an optional name if you enter one, and which hole you were on. Only send what you're happy for us to read.

Where the data goes

Play statistics and feedback are stored in a private Google Sheet used only by Cnmbuck Studios to improve the game. They aren't shared with or sold to anyone else.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages and doesn't knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

Questions or deletion requests

Email cnmbuckstudios@gmail.com. To have your feedback or play statistics deleted, tell us roughly when you played and the device you used. Changes to this policy will be posted here.
