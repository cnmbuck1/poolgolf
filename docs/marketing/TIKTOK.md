- `rack-and-roll-tiktok.mp4`: ends with "Free on the App Store". Post once the app is live.
- `rack-and-roll-tiktok-coming-soon.mp4`: ends with "Coming soon to the App Store", for posting before launch.

**Tips**

  original post.

  "rate my trick shot", "Pro mode with no aiming guide".
